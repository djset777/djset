travel

This part of the Dominican Republic is lush and quietly rooted in the heart of the island: nothing like the resort towns most visitors know. If you have any questions about getting here or getting around, just reach out. We're happy to help however we can.

getting in

We recommend flying into Santiago International Airport (STI): the closest airport and the most practical entry point. From there, La Vega is about 45 minutes by car.

entry requirements

A valid passport is required for the full duration of your stay. An E-Ticket is also required before departure and before leaving the DR: complete it for free at eticket.migracion.gob.do.

passport

Check your expiration date before booking. We recommend it be valid at least six months beyond July 7, 2027. Standard renewal takes 6–8 weeks; expedited renewal takes 2–3 weeks.

getting around

We recommend arranging a private transfer or rental car in advance. Rideshares are not reliably available in this part of the island. Reach out if you'd like recommendations.

where to stay

We've reserved a private block of cabanas for out-of-town guests: details coming soon. Guests staying at the cabanas will be included in the wedding day shuttle to and from the venue. If you prefer to arrange your own accommodations, we're happy to share recommendations on request.

currency

The Dominican Republic uses the Dominican Peso (DOP). US dollars are widely accepted, but pesos are helpful for smaller purchases. ATMs are available in La Vega.

q&a

When is the RSVP deadline?
Please RSVP by March 27, 2027 so we can plan thoughtfully for everyone joining us. If your plans change, please let us know as soon as you can.
May I bring a guest?
Your invitation reflects the space we have reserved. If a guest is included, their name will appear alongside yours.
Are kids welcome?
Our celebration will be adults-only. We hope this gives you the chance to enjoy the evening fully: let us know if we can help with planning.
Does your wedding have a theme?
This is a celebration of not only our love but our land. Think outdoor jungle garden party: deep greens, ocean blues, warm gold.
What should I wear?
Creative Black Tie in shades of blue and/or green. We ask that all guests dress within our palette so the day feels whole. Inspiration on our Pinterest. The terrain includes grass and natural pathways: we recommend wedges, block heels, or elegant flats.
Are there any colors to avoid?
Out of respect for the bride, please avoid white, ivory, and cream. We also ask that guests dress within our palette of deep greens, ocean blues, and warm gold.
Where should I park?
Valet service will be available on-site: please follow the signs when you arrive.
Is there transportation between reception and hotels?
Guests staying at our reserved block of cabanas will be included in the wedding day shuttle.
What will the weather be like?
Warm, lush, and alive: expect sunshine, heat, and humidity. Lightweight fabrics and breathable textures will keep you comfortable. In the event of rain, covered areas are provided.
Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?
Our ceremony and reception will take place outdoors, surrounded by nature, with covered areas provided for comfort.
Can I take pictures during the wedding?
We kindly ask that our celebration remain fully unplugged. Please keep phones, cameras, and recording devices away throughout the ceremony and reception. We've poured our hearts into creating an atmosphere where everyone can be fully present. Thank you for honoring this request.
