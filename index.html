La Vega, Dominican Republic

Where the hills are green, the nights are alive, and the love is deep.

Travel

This part of the Dominican Republic is lush and quietly rooted in the heart of the island: nothing like the resort towns most visitors know. We're so excited to share it with you, and if you have any questions about getting here or getting around, just reach out. We're happy to help however we can.

Getting In
We recommend flying into Santiago International Airport (STI): it's the closest airport and the most practical entry point. From there, La Vega is about 45 minutes by car.
Entry Requirements
A valid passport is required for the full duration of your stay. An E-Ticket is also required before departure and before leaving the DR: you can complete it for free at eticket.migracion.gob.do.
Passport
Check your expiration date before booking. We recommend it be valid at least six months beyond July 7, 2027. Standard renewal takes 6–8 weeks; expedited renewal takes 2–3 weeks.
Getting Around
We recommend arranging a private transfer or rental car in advance. Rideshares are not reliably available in this part of the island. Reach out if you'd like recommendations.
Where to Stay
We've reserved a private block of cabanas for out-of-town guests: details coming soon. Guests staying at the cabanas will be included in the wedding day shuttle to and from the venue. If you prefer to arrange your own accommodations, we're happy to share recommendations on request.
Currency
The Dominican Republic uses the Dominican Peso (DOP). US dollars are widely accepted, but pesos are helpful for smaller purchases. ATMs are available in La Vega.
Things to Do
There's plenty to explore nearby: horseback riding to Salto Baiguate, paragliding, coffee farm tours, white-water rafting, four wheels and ATV, canyoning, and river trekking.

Exploration beyond La Vega is entirely at guests' own discretion. Recommendations available on request.
